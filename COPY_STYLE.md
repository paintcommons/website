# Paint Commons copy style

Use the concise, descriptive style of Wikipedia’s main navigation pages and multilingual portal, rather than article prose or promotional writing. Reference pages: https://www.wikipedia.org/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Page, and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Contents. Article excerpts on these pages are not copy models.

- Use sentence case for headings, navigation, controls, and field labels. Retain proper names, manufacturer spellings, product codes, and standard abbreviations.
- Do not add full stops to headings or short labels. Use normal punctuation for complete sentences.
- Use the Oxford comma in lists of three or more items. Write “and” instead of an ampersand unless it belongs to a proper name.
- Use British spelling in site-authored text: catalogue, colour, grey, and behaviour. Preserve names and source quotations as published.
- State what a page contains or an action does. Avoid slogans, rhetorical questions outside FAQs, promotional claims, and strings of sentence fragments.
- Keep calculated colour families separate from manufacturer-published families. Show “Not recorded” for missing factual information.
- Preserve product names and source descriptions; copy editing must not change catalogue data.

The website’s spelling convention is separate from Stipplewise’s US/UK language setting.
